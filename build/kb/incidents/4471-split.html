The three problems

parser · the shared parser reads every date month first

A day-first tenant writes the 7th of April as 07/04. The shared parser reads it as 4 July. The report shows the same two numbers, swapped: “Our 7 April reminder went out on 4 July.” It reaches every day-first tenant through the app, email and the booking-v2 form. Ticket #1, fixed: fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales.

hint · booking-v3 tells people to type the month first

The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, so people type the month first, as told. The form then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The report looks the same as a parser report, so reading it does not tell them apart: only the tenant’s form and the form channel do. The parser fix does not touch this one. The fix is the hint, or how the form reads it. Ticket #2, won’t fix: the booking-v3 form is retired.

one-day · the date moves by one day

These reports show no swap. A Tuesday turns into a Monday. A client in Lisbon gets Tuesday and a client in Toronto gets Monday for the same meeting. An invite arrives before the booking was made. The date shifts by one day, the way a time-zone or midnight conversion shifts it. The desk noted these reports in desk-notes.json but counted them under parser by tenant. Ticket #3, open: the engineering update does not cover it.

How the split was made

  • One id, one report. The export repeats some reports across its pages. Duplicates are dropped.
  • Cause by tenant, not by intake. The intake tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser. It disagrees with the tenant in both the parser and the hint groups.
  • Bodies read, not only subjects. Decision 0001 merges on the subject alone. Most subjects in #4471 appear under more than one problem, so the subject cannot tell the problems apart.
